I am a final year PhD Student in the Department of Computer Science at Yale University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Dan Spielman. In the summer of 2026, I was a Research Intern at Sakana AI, hosted by Robert T. Lange. Prior to this, I completed a MSc in Data Science under the supervision of Luca Trevisan at Bocconi University. Even earlier, I completed a BSc in economics and computer science under the supervision of Fabio Angelo Maccheroni.

I am broadly interested in theoretical computer science, in particular spectral graph theory and the design and analysis of approximation algorithms. More recently, I have been interested in combining the lenses of theory and LLM-guided automated discovery for the design of practical algorithms, particularly for problems in numerical linear algebra and optimization.

I am looking for postdocs or industry research positions starting Summer/Fall 2027
