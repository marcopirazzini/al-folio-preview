Our new paper, [A Fast and Accurate Algorithm for the $p \to q$ Norm of Non-Negative Matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09601) is now available on arXiv. Our algorithm is based on an Interior Point Method which also has [a practical implementation](https://github.com/marcopirazzini/MatrixNormIPM.jl).
